The Three Layers

Starter guide for best-minds-drill · about 15 minutes · 10 exercises

/best-minds-drill reads your own Claude Code transcripts, diagnoses how you write prompts against three layers, and drills you on what it finds. But it assumes you already know the three layers. If you don't, its exercises can only be solved by reading the answers.

This guide is the missing first step: what each layer is, how it shows up in a real prompt, and ten exercises that tell you whether you're ready.

Layer 1 · Spec

Before the work starts, say what you want, why, and how much of it. Spec = purpose + boundary. One without the other doesn't count.

  • Purpose: the decision the work feeds. “Write a report” is a task. “Decide where next quarter's ad budget goes” is a purpose.
  • Boundary: how big, where it stops, what's out of scope.

Scenario. You run operations at a small company. Your manager asks you to “take a look at last quarter's sales.”

❌ WeakAnalyze last quarter's sales data
✅ StrongAnalyze last quarter's sales data. I need to decide which channel gets more of next quarter's ad budget. Only the three online channels — search, social, email; ignore in-store. If the data can't support a conclusion, tell me what's missing instead of forcing one.
FragmentWhat it does
Analyze last quarter's sales dataThe task — the deliverable
I need to decide which channel…Purpose. Decides what to calculate (return per channel) and what not to.
Only the three online channelsBoundary. Decides when the work is done.
Tell me what's missing instead of forcing oneSurfaces assumptions. Every assumption the AI makes silently is a chance to drift.
The one-line testCross out the deliverable. Is a decision left?
“Analyze sales data” → nothing left. Spec is missing.

Same task, different purpose, different work: if the purpose were “decide whether to let a sales rep go”, you'd calculate completely different things. The AI can't choose your purpose for you — but it can help you find it. When you're unsure, ask it to interview you before it starts.

第 1 层 · Spec

开工之前，说清楚你要什么、为什么要、做多大。Spec = 目的 + 边界。少一个都不算。

  • 目的：这件事服务于哪个决定。「写一份报告」是任务；「决定下季度广告预算往哪放」是目的。
  • 边界：做多大、做到哪为止、哪些不管。

情景。你在一家小公司做运营，老板让你「看看上季度的销售数据」。

❌ 弱帮我分析一下上季度的销售数据
✅ 强帮我分析上季度的销售数据。我要决定的是下季度的广告预算往哪个渠道多放。只看线上三个渠道：搜索、社交、邮件，线下门店不用管。数据不够下结论的话，直接告诉我缺什么，不要硬凑。
片段作用
分析上季度的销售数据任务，也就是交付物
我要决定的是……往哪个渠道多放目的。决定了该算什么（各渠道回报），不该算什么。
只看线上三个渠道边界。决定了什么时候算做完。
缺什么就直说，不要硬凑让假设浮上来。AI 默默做的每个假设，都是跑偏的机会。
一句话检验把交付物的名字划掉，还剩一个决定吗？
「帮我分析销售数据」→ 什么都没剩。缺 Spec。

同样的任务，目的不同，活就不同：如果目的是「决定要不要裁掉一个销售」，要算的东西完全不一样。AI 替你选不了目的，但能帮你把目的问出来——拿不准的时候，让它先问你几个问题再动手。

Layer 2 · Verifier

After delivery, what will you compare the result against to know it's right? Write that down before the work starts.

  • Checkable: an action you can take or a thing you can count. “Make it professional” isn't checkable. “At most five bullets per section” is.
  • Outside signals first: the AI saying “deployed successfully” doesn't count. Opening the URL does.
  • Up front: every criterion you invent after the draft arrives costs another round trip.

Scenario. You ask the AI to write this week's project update for a client.

❌ WeakWrite a project update for the client, make it professional
✅ StrongWrite this week's project update for the client. Three sections: done this week, planned next week, decisions we need from the client. At most five bullets per section. Every decision item states a deadline and what happens if nobody decides. Check the draft against these three rules before you send it to me.
FragmentWhat it does
make it professional✗ An adjective. Nothing to compare against.
Three sections: …Structure criterion. Countable.
At most five bulletsQuantity criterion. Countable.
A deadline and a consequence per itemContent criterion. Tick it off item by item.
Check against these three rulesA self-check — useful only because the rules are checkable.
The one-line testCould someone else judge whether it's right, using only this sentence?
“Professional” — no; everyone's version differs. “At most five bullets” — yes.

“Check your own work” is not a Verifier on its own. Self-checking against “professional” checks nothing. It works only when the criteria are checkable — and for anything that matters, use a real signal: run it, open it, ask the person it's for.

第 2 层 · Verifier

交付之后，你拿什么去比，才知道做对了。这个标准要在开工前就写下来。

  • 能检查：做完之后能去做的动作，或者能数的东西。「专业一点」不能检查，「每段不超过 5 条」能。
  • 外部信号优先：AI 说「部署成功」不算数，打开地址看一眼才算。
  • 提前写：交付后才想出来的标准，每一条都要多一个来回。

情景。你让 AI 帮你写这周给客户的项目周报。

❌ 弱帮我写一份项目周报，写得专业一点
✅ 强帮我写这周给客户的项目周报。分三段：本周完成、下周计划、需要客户决定的事。每段不超过 5 条。「需要客户决定的事」每条写明截止日期，以及不决定的后果。写完对照这三条自己检查一遍再给我。
片段作用
写得专业一点✗ 形容词，做完没东西可比。
分三段：……结构标准。能数。
每段不超过 5 条数量标准。能数。
每条写明截止日期和后果内容标准。能逐条打勾。
对照这三条检查自查——只因为标准能检查，它才有用。
一句话检验只凭这句话，别人能不能替你判断做没做对？
「专业一点」不能，每个人心里的专业不一样。「每段不超过 5 条」能。

让 AI「自己检查一遍」本身不是 Verifier。对着「专业一点」自查，等于没查。自查只有在标准能检查时才有意义；重要的事，找真实信号：跑一下、打开看、问问用它的人。

Layer 3 · Environment

Next time, what should hold without you saying it again? Environment isn't content — it's location. The same sentence typed in the chat box is a prompt. Written into CLAUDE.md, it's environment.

  • CLAUDE.md: rules loaded automatically every session. For “always do it this way.”
  • Skill: a reusable procedure. For “this kind of task always goes like this.”
  • Hook: a check that runs when the AI calls a tool, and can block the call. For “this must never happen.”

Scenario. Every time you ask the AI to change code, you remind it of two things: run the tests afterwards, and don't touch the database migration files. This is the fourth time this week.

❌ Weak — the fourth timeFix the error message on the login page. Usual rules: run the tests after, and don't touch the migration files.
✅ StrongMove the rules into CLAUDE.md at the project root: 
## Rules
- After changing code, run npm test and paste the result
- Never modify anything under db/migrations/
Now the prompt is just: Fix the error message on the login page.
FragmentWhat it does
Usual rules✗ A repetition signal. This belongs somewhere else.
run the tests → CLAUDE.mdAn “always do” — goes in the rules file.
don't touch migrations → CLAUDE.md, or a hookA “never do” — a rule; if a slip would be costly, enforce it with a hook.

Sort rules by the cost of a mistake:

TierExampleWhere it lives
Always doRun the tests after a changeCLAUDE.md
Ask firstAdd a new dependencyCLAUDE.md, saying “ask me first”
Never doEdit migrations, delete production dataCLAUDE.md + a hook
The one-line testWill this still be true next time? How many times have I said it?

Why a hook for “never”? A rule in CLAUDE.md is a request: the AI can miss or ignore it. A hook is a rule: it can block the tool call as it happens. But hooks only guard tool calls — editing files, running commands. They can't govern what the AI says, so a rule like “don't make things up” is out of a hook's reach. See CLAUDE.md and hooks in the Claude Code docs.

第 3 层 · Environment

下一次，什么东西不用你再说一遍。Environment 不是内容，是位置。同一句话写在聊天框里是提示词，写进 CLAUDE.md 就是环境。

  • CLAUDE.md：每次会话自动加载的规则。适合「每次都该这样」的事。
  • Skill：可复用的一整套流程。适合「这类任务每次都这么做」。
  • Hook：AI 调用工具时自动运行的检查，可以直接拦下这次调用。适合「绝对不能发生」的事。

情景。你每次让 AI 改代码，都要提醒两件事：改完跑测试；不要动数据库迁移文件。这周已经是第四次了。

❌ 弱（第四次）改一下登录页的报错提示。老规矩：改完跑测试，别动数据库迁移文件。
✅ 强把规则挪进项目根目录的 CLAUDE.md： 
## 规则
- 改完代码必须运行 npm test，并贴出结果
- 不要修改 db/migrations/ 下的任何文件
然后提示词只剩：改一下登录页的报错提示。
片段作用
老规矩✗ 重复信号。这句话本该住在别处。
改完跑测试 → CLAUDE.md「每次都该做」，写进规则文件。
别动迁移文件 → CLAUDE.md，或 hook「绝对不能做」，写成规则；出错代价高，就用 hook 强制拦截。

按做错的代价分三档：

档位例子放哪
总是做改完跑测试CLAUDE.md
先问我引入新的依赖包CLAUDE.md，写明「先问我」
绝不做改迁移文件、删生产数据CLAUDE.md + hook
一句话检验这句话下次还成立吗？这是我第几次说了？

为什么「绝不做」要用 hook？写在 CLAUDE.md 里的规则是请求，AI 可能漏看或忽略；hook 是规则，能在工具调用发生的那一刻直接拦下。但 hook 只管得住工具调用（改文件、跑命令），管不住 AI 说什么——「不要编造信息」这类规则，hook 帮不上。详见 Claude Code 文档里的 CLAUDE.md 和 hooks。

Telling them apart

Spec and Verifier blur the most, because they often share a sentence. Separate them by time:

  • Spec is what's needed before the work — or nobody knows what to do.
  • Verifier is what's needed after delivery — or nobody knows whether it's done.

One sentence doing both is a good thing. “At most five bullets per section” sets a boundary (Spec) and gives you something to count (Verifier). Environment is about location: the question isn't “what does this say?” but “will it still be true next time?”

The procedure. Ask in order — the first hit is the answer.

  1. Is this repeating something said before? → yes → Environment
  2. Cross out the deliverable. Is a decision left? → no → Spec
  3. When it's done, what will it be compared against? → can't say → Verifier
  4. All three pass → all three present

Three more rules:

  • Acceptance turns (“ok”, “pass”, “looks good”) don't assign work. Skip question 2 and go straight to question 3.
  • Not applicable isn't missing. For a one-off task, Environment simply doesn't apply. Not every prompt is broken — don't hunt for a flaw.
  • A signal is a pause, not a verdict. When you catch “since X, then Y”, ask: can I check this premise? Checkable but unchecked is a defect. Uncheckable — it needs knowledge you don't have — carry on, but have the AI list the possibilities first, so the search has a stopping point.

And: longer isn't more complete. Trading adjectives for actions usually makes a prompt shorter.

怎么区分

最容易混的是 Spec 和 Verifier，因为它们常写在同一句话里。分开它们看时间：

  • Spec 是开工前就要知道的——不然不知道该做什么。
  • Verifier 是交付后才用得上的——不然不知道做完了没有。

一句话占两层是好事。「每段不超过 5 条」既划了边界（Spec），又能拿去数（Verifier）。Environment 看位置：问的不是「这句话说了什么」，而是「它下次还成立吗」。

判定顺序。按顺序问，第一个命中的就是答案。

  1. 这句话在重复之前说过的东西吗？→ 是 → Environment
  2. 把交付物划掉，还剩一个决定吗？→ 没剩 → Spec
  3. 做完之后，拿什么去比？→ 说不出来 → Verifier
  4. 三问都过 → 三层都在

再加三条：

  • 验收时说的话（「好」「pass」「就这样吧」）不是在布置任务。跳过第 2 问，直接问第 3 问。
  • 不适用不等于缺失。一次性的小任务，Environment 本来就不适用。不是每条提示词都有毛病，别硬找。
  • 信号是停一秒的提醒，不是判决。看到自己写出「既然 X，那么 Y」，先问：这个前提我验得了吗？验得了却没验，是缺陷；验不了（需要你不具备的知识），照常往下走，但先让 AI 把所有可能性列出来，当作停止条件。

还有：长不等于完整。把形容词换成动作，提示词往往更短。

10 exercises

Six ask which layer is missing; four ask which fix is best. They're deliberately mixed rather than grouped by layer — telling confusable categories apart is exactly what mixed practice trains. Feedback depends on the option you pick. Keep the procedure above open while you work: looking it up is the point at this stage.

10 道练习

六道问缺哪一层，四道问哪个改法最好。题目故意打乱、不按层分组——把容易混的类别分开，正是交错练习最擅长的事。反馈会根据你选的选项给出。做题时可以开着上面的判定顺序，这个阶段查表就是该做的事。

Where this comes from

Read one thing: Andrej Karpathy's own summary of his Sequoia Ascent 2026 talk. It ends with the line this whole guide rests on: “You can outsource your thinking, but you can't outsource your understanding.”

The three-layer framing was popularized by a video that repackaged that talk. A few of the video's claims don't hold, and this guide doesn't use them:

  • “Validation is the only real lever.” All three layers are levers. Karpathy's actual line — “Traditional software automates what you can specify. LLMs and reinforcement learning automate what you can verify” — is about which tasks get automated, not advice on writing prompts.
  • CLAUDE.md, skills and hooks don't appear in Karpathy's summary; that layer is the video's addition. It's sound practice, but its authority is the Claude Code documentation, not Karpathy.
  • The “robot librarian” picture of AI misleads: models generalize rather than look things up, which is also why they produce believable wrong answers. What makes a Verifier worth having is whether an answer can be checked.

Why best-minds-drill then practices on your transcripts instead of examples like these: knowing a rule and noticing when it applies are different abilities. Experts' knowledge comes with the conditions for using it attached — see Bransford, Brown & Cocking, How People Learn, ch. 2. This guide gives you the rules; the drill trains the noticing.

这些说法从哪来

只读一个就够：Andrej Karpathy 本人对 Sequoia Ascent 2026 演讲的摘要。它的最后一句，是这整份指南的立足点：“You can outsource your thinking, but you can't outsource your understanding.”（思考可以外包，理解不能。）

三层这个框架是由一段解读这场演讲的视频流行起来的。视频里有几处说法站不住，这份指南没有采用：

  • 「验证是唯一真正的杠杆」。三层都是杠杆。Karpathy 的原话是 “Traditional software automates what you can specify. LLMs and reinforcement learning automate what you can verify”——讲的是哪些任务会被自动化，不是写提示词的建议。
  • CLAUDE.md、skill、hook 在 Karpathy 的摘要里没有出现，第三层是视频加的。做法本身是对的，但它的权威是 Claude Code 官方文档，不是 Karpathy。
  • 把 AI 比作「机器人图书管理员」会误导：模型是泛化，不是查书，这也正是它能编出可信错答案的原因。决定要不要 Verifier 的，是答案能不能被检查。

为什么 best-minds-drill 接下来要用你自己的对话记录练，而不是用这里的例子：知道一条规则，和在该用的时候想起来用，是两种能力。专家的知识自带「什么时候用」的条件——见 Bransford、Brown 与 Cocking 主编的 How People Learn 第 2 章。这份指南给你规则，drill 训练的是想起来。
